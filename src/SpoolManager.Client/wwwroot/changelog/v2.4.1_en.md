# v2.4.1 - Orca Export for Your Spools

**Release Date:** 2026-07-12

## New Features
- **Export your spools to Orca Slicer**: The Orca export page now shows a card with the materials of your current spools, so you can export exactly the filament profiles you own with one click
- **"On spool" indicator**: The materials list shows which materials are currently on one of your active spools

## Improvements
- The materials list can be sorted by every column
