# v2.6.0 - The Big Redesign

**Release Date:** 2026-11-25

## New Design
- **Completely redesigned interface** with a calm, modern look – every page was rebuilt
- **Spools as rings**: every spool is shown as a ring in its real filament colour – the filled part shows what is left
- **Light, dark or system**: pick your theme in the sidebar or menu; "System" follows your device
- **New navigation**: sidebar on desktop, a bottom bar with a big scan button on phones, and a global search (Ctrl K)
- **Dashboard that tells you what to do**: almost empty spools, finished drying and spools without a place appear as cards with the next action
- **New shelf view** with filters for material, owner, place and status, plus "by material" grouping
- **Spool page**: weigh with a visible calculation, move the spool with one tap, see its history
- **Scan screen**: scan a spool, then a printer or shelf to move it – or open a tag file without NFC
- **Locations, materials, projects, settings and admin pages** redesigned and made consistent
- **New public pages**: landing page, sign in, registration and password reset in the new look, with theme and language switch

## Improvements
- Clear error messages instead of technical responses
- Confirmation before deleting, empty states with a helpful next step everywhere
- Works great on phones – no more sideways scrolling
- Better accessibility: larger touch targets, labels for screen readers, visible keyboard focus

## Bug Fixes
- The global search now finds spools by colour, brand, place or owner
- Dates and drying times are shown in your local time
- Materials that are still used by spools can no longer be deleted by accident
- Many smaller fixes found while rebuilding every page
